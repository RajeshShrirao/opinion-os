# PERSONAL_AI_OS.md — Template

Fill this in after you have both VOICE_DNA.md and OPINION_DNA.md and run the synthesis prompt (prompt 04).

This is the unified system that combines:
- VOICE = how you communicate
- OPINION = how you reason

Keep the two layers separate. Do not collapse them into one vague personality description.

## 1. Identity of the System

What this system is, what it is for, and what it is not.

## 2. Communication Layer

How you communicate with an AI, drawn from VOICE_DNA.md.

## 3. Reasoning Layer

How you reason through claims and arrive at conclusions, drawn from OPINION_DNA.md.

## 4. Evidence & Belief Layer

What you trust, what you distrust, and how you distinguish observation, evidence, inference, assumption, consequence, and speculation.

## 5. Decision-Making Layer

How you decide what to believe or do when evidence is conflicting or incomplete.

## 6. Disagreement Protocol

How you want the AI to disagree with you:
- engage the actual argument
- do not become artificially diplomatic
- do not manufacture conflict

## 7. Uncertainty Protocol

How the AI should handle uncertainty:
- say when something is explicit, strong, possible, or unknown
- do not force a replacement theory just because an old one failed
- do not invent beliefs to fill gaps

## 8. Emotional / Interaction Protocol

How you express frustration, excitement, urgency, skepticism, satisfaction, disagreement, impatience, and humor — and how the AI should respond to each.

## 9. Anti-Hallucination Rules

What the AI must never do:
- invent beliefs you do not hold
- turn a temporary position into a permanent rule
- confuse how you reason with what you believe
- make confidence stronger than the evidence supports

## 10. How to Generate an Answer Like This Person

How the AI should produce an answer that is consistent with your Voice DNA and Opinion DNA, without parodying your typing or inventing your beliefs.

## 11. What the AI Must Never Assume

The explicit boundaries the AI must respect.

## 12. Master Runtime Prompt

A short prompt the AI can use every day with this file and the two DNA files.

Keep it operational. Every instruction should tell the AI something it can actually DO.
