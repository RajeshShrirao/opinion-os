# VOICE_DNA.md — Template

Fill this in after running the VOICE DNA Analyzer (prompt 01).

This file describes how you communicate with an AI.

IMPORTANT:
This is a behavioral fingerprint, not a writing sample.
It should describe HOW you communicate, not what you know or believe.

## 1. Voice Summary

A short paragraph describing the overall shape of your communication with an AI.

Focus on the mechanisms, not vague labels.

## 2. Core Communication Rules

The most important recurring rules in how you communicate.

Example structure:
- Rule 1 — ...
- Rule 2 — ...
- Rule 3 — ...

## 3. Sentence & Rhythm Patterns

How your messages are built:
- short-message patterns
- long-message patterns
- fragments
- chained thoughts
- self-corrections
- question stacking
- how rhythm changes by task

## 4. Vocabulary & Phrasing

Recurring words, phrases, abbreviations, shorthand, and the linguistic mechanisms you use to express things.

## 5. Formatting Habits

Capitalization, punctuation, apostrophes, commas, paragraphs, bullets, lowercase usage, spacing around punctuation, message length.

## 6. Personality & Delivery

How you express:
- frustration
- excitement
- urgency
- skepticism
- satisfaction
- disagreement
- impatience
- humor

Identify the linguistic mechanisms, not just the labels.

## 7. What I Never Do

Things your history actually shows you rarely or never do.

Only include these if the evidence supports them.

## 8. Signature Patterns

Repeated:
- sentence openers
- phrases
- abbreviations
- transitions
- question structures
- correction structures
- reasoning structures

Separate:
HIGH-CONFIDENCE recurring patterns
MEDIUM-CONFIDENCE patterns
WEAK / POSSIBLE patterns

## 9. Before and After Examples

Show what your raw messages look like, and what a wrong imitation would look like, so the difference is clear.

## 10. Replication Prompt

A short prompt another AI can use to recognize and reproduce your prompting style from this file.

Do not make the person sound more polished than they actually are.
