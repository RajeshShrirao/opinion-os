# OPINION_DNA.md — Template

Fill this in after running the Opinion DNA Interview (prompts 02 and 03).

This file describes how you form opinions, judgments, beliefs, and conclusions.

IMPORTANT:
This file models your reasoning PROCESS, not your specific beliefs.
Do not turn individual opinions on particular subjects into general rules.

## 1. Core Thinking Principles

The most important principles in how you think through claims and arrive at conclusions.

## 2. How I Form Opinions

The sequence or system you use to form an opinion:
- what you do first
- what you do next
- what would change your mind
- how you handle uncertainty

## 3. Evidence Hierarchy

What you trust, in order:
- repeated real-world consequences
- evidence that survives contact with reality
- personal observation
- trusted people / experts
- public opinion
- something else

Only include what your answers actually support.

## 4. Skepticism & Contrarian Rules

How skeptical you are, whether you are naturally contrarian, how you react to popular opinions, and how you handle disagreement.

## 5. Claim Strength & Uncertainty

How strongly you make claims, how much uncertainty you tolerate, and how you distinguish confidence from certainty.

## 6. What Changes My Mind

The mechanisms that actually change your opinions:
- repeated consequences
- evidence
- accumulation
- no replacement required
- something else

## 7. How I Generate Interesting Angles

How you come up with non-obvious takes, if you do.

## 8. Opinion Boundaries

What the AI must NOT invent for you:
- beliefs you do not actually hold
- permanent beliefs from temporary positions
- contrarian opinions from skepticism
- false certainty from uncertainty

## 9. Step-by-Step Framework for Forming a New Opinion Like Me

An operational version of your reasoning system that another AI can follow.

## 10. Short Prompt for Another AI

A short prompt another AI can use with this file to reason in a way compatible with how you think.

At the end, explicitly list what is still UNKNOWN about your reasoning system.
